Pill button with built-in hover lift (1px) and press shrink (0.96, 100ms); use one primary per view.

```jsx
<Button size="lg" onClick={explode}>Take it apart</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">Skip</Button>
<Button variant="onStudio">Reset view</Button>
```

- `variant`: primary | secondary | onStudio
- `size`: md (48px) | lg (52px)
- Sentence-case labels, verb first.
